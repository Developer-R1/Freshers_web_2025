About Freshers Day

Welcome to NIT PY's Freshers Day - the most awaited event of the year! This is where new beginnings meet endless possibilities, and the journey of camaraderie, creativity, and fun begins. Freshers Day is a celebration of new friendships, unforgettable memories, and the spirit of togetherness as we welcome the newest members of our NIT PY family.

From thrilling games and exciting competitions to cultural performances and interactive sessions, this day is packed with activities designed to bring out the best in everyone. It's a time to showcase your talents, connect with peers, and create memories that will last a lifetime.

Whether you're a participant or a spectator, Freshers Day promises an experience like no other. So, gear up to dive into the excitement, embrace the challenges, and celebrate the start of an incredible journey at NIT PY. Let's make this day unforgettable!
